IsKompyut: A Web Application for Personal Performance Monitoring to Improve Academic Awareness Among PUP Scholars

Address the issues of grade uncertainty and academic stress, this study proposes the development of IsKompyut, a specialized web application tailored for the PUP academic environment.

Guide

Watch the video below to get started with IsKompyut. This short guide will walk you through how to navigate the system, input your academic data, and understand your performance results. It is designed to help users maximize the features of IsKompyut for better academic awareness and monitoring.

About Us

IsKompyut is a web-based academic performance monitoring system designed for Polytechnic University of the Philippines (PUP) scholars. It was developed to address common student concerns such as grade uncertainty, lack of real-time academic tracking, and the stress that comes with not having a clear view of academic standing.

The system provides a simple and organized platform where students can encode their grades, view computed results, and monitor their academic progress per subject. With its user-friendly interface, IsKompyut makes it easier for students to stay updated with their performance throughout the semester without confusion or unnecessary complexity.

Beyond grade tracking, IsKompyut also offers performance insights that help students better understand their strengths and areas for improvement. It aims to support smarter academic decisions, encourage self-awareness, and promote a more confident and less stressful learning experience for PUP scholars.
